Forensic Notes: Health Information

Good fitness is 30% workout and 70% lifestyle. Study the evidence.

🥗

Balanced Nutrition

Eat protein (dal, paneer, eggs, chicken), complex carbs (oats, brown rice, roti) and healthy fats (nuts, seeds). Avoid too much sugar and fried food.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink 2.5 to 3.5 litres of water daily. Drink more on workout days and in hot weather.

😴

Sleep & Recovery

Sleep 7 to 9 hours every night. Muscles grow during rest, not during the workout. Take 1 or 2 rest days each week.

🧘

Warm-up & Cool-down

Warm up for 5 to 10 minutes before lifting and stretch for 5 minutes after. This prevents injuries.
